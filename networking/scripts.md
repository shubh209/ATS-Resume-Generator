## #2 — Warm contact referral ask
**Platform:** LinkedIn DM / text
**Use when:** You know them personally (ASU, internship, events)

```
Hey [Name],

Hope things are going well! I just graduated from ASU with my MS in CS and I'm actively looking for SWE roles.

I noticed you're at [Company] — I've been really interested in them, especially [specific thing about company/team]. Do you happen to know if they're hiring for SWE roles, and would you feel comfortable referring me if there's a fit?

No pressure at all — just thought I'd reach out since I trust your judgment.

Thanks!
```

## #3 — Cold LinkedIn connection note
**Platform:** LinkedIn (300 char hard limit, exactly 3 lines, no greeting)
**Use when:** First contact, no prior relationship

> **Use the dedicated prompt:** `prompts/linkedin-connection-note.md`  
> Intents: `network` | `ask` | `referral` (paste JD for referral). Cursor skill: `.cursor/skills/linkedin-connection-note/SKILL.md`

**Structure:**
- Line 1 — Who Shubh is (tie background to their role at Company)
- Line 2 — One project + one metric from `networking/candidate-profile.md`
- Line 3 — `Would love to connect.` (network) · calendar ask (ask) · referral ask (referral + JD)

Do not include OPT/H1-B in the 300-char note. Apply `reference/humanizer.md`.
