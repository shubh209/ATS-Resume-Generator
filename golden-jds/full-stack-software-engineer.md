## Software Engineer, Full Stack

### About the role

We are hiring a Full Stack Software Engineer to build customer facing web applications and the APIs behind them. You will work with product and design in two week Agile sprints.

### Required qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field (or equivalent experience)
- 1+ years experience with **TypeScript** and **JavaScript**
- Strong **HTML/CSS** and modern **React** development
- Experience building **REST APIs** and working with **SQL** databases
- Familiarity with **cloud** platforms (**AWS**, Azure, or GCP)
- **Git** version control and **CI/CD** pipelines
- **DevOps** mindset: Docker, automated deploys, monitoring
- Ability to **collaborate** with cross functional teams and **communicate** tradeoffs to non technical stakeholders

### Preferred qualifications

- **Node.js** backend frameworks (Express, Fastify, or similar)
- Experience with **Redis** or job queues for async workflows
- Mobile or responsive web experience

### Responsibilities

- Design and implement full stack features from UI through database
- Write unit and integration tests; participate in code review
- Deploy services to cloud infrastructure and support production health
- Take feedback from senior engineers and iterate quickly
