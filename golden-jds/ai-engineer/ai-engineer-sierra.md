## AI Engineer / Agent Engineer (Sierra)

### About the role

Sierra builds a platform for companies to create AI-powered customer experiences, partnering with
enterprises like SoftBank, Uber, Rivian, CLEAR, and Sutter Health. Primarily in-person in San
Francisco. Co-founded by Bret Taylor (Board Chair of OpenAI, ex co-CEO of Salesforce, ex CTO of
Facebook) and Clay Bavor (ex Google Labs). Values: Trust, Customer Obsession, Craftsmanship,
Intensity, Family.

### What you'll do

- Design and deliver production-grade AI agents: mission-critical, scalable systems running in
  production across finance, healthcare, and commerce, not prototypes.
- Drive the Agent Development Life Cycle (ADLC) end to end: full ownership from pilot through
  deployment and continuous iteration, defining ADLC best practices.
- Partner directly with large enterprises and startups to understand business challenges and build
  agents that transform how they operate at scale.
- Build the future of the platform: surface unmet customer needs, prototype tools and features, and
  collaborate with research, product, and platform teams.

### Example projects

- AI agents for telecom/media companies that outperform human agents at managing subscription churn.
- Agents navigating complex interactions like device troubleshooting and personalized recommendations.
- Generalizable, industry-specific AI agent frameworks (e.g. financial services).
- Design partnerships for new agent architectures, self-service, and generative agent development.
- Integrating the latest voice models at enterprise scale.

### What you'll bring

- Experience building and scaling end-to-end production systems.
- Strong technical problem-solving in fast-changing, ambiguous environments.
- Builder/tinkerer mindset with high agency; find creative ways to ship past obstacles.
- Comfort working directly with customers to understand needs and solve real problems.
- Excellent communication: clear, direct, persuasive across technical and non-technical audiences.

### Even better

- Building or deploying AI/LLM systems in production.
- Founder or founding-engineer experience (balancing craft, ownership, speed).
- Familiarity with AI agent tooling: eval frameworks, agent tooling, RAG pipelines, prompt engineering.
- React, TypeScript, and/or Go.
- Prior customer-facing roles or leading technical projects with external stakeholders.
