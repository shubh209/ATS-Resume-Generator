## Artificial Intelligence Specialist — The Phoenix Group

### Role overview

Assists in evaluating, testing, and deploying generative AI solutions across legal and business functions. Supports AI pilots, prompt testing, workflow documentation, and user adoption for legal-technology initiatives in a corporate/law-firm environment.

### Key responsibilities (abridged)

- Observe/document/analyze legal and business workflows to find AI-integration and automation opportunities.
- Develop, test, and refine prompts for legal and operational use cases.
- Execute pilot programs for approved AI/legal-tech platforms; organize test cases, record results, escalate issues.
- Maintain documentation of prompts, testing outcomes, limitations, and use cases.
- Collaborate on lightweight AI apps and workflow automation via low-code/no-code platforms (contract review, legal research, matter intake, billing, document management, compliance).
- Serve as point of contact for end-users: training, user guides, FAQs, firm-wide AI adoption.
- Organize firm-wide AI events (logistics, content, tracking). Manage AI content databases.

### Core qualifications

- Bachelor's in Business, Legal Studies, Data, Communications, or Technology; 1-2 years preferred.
- Foundational experience with generative AI tools, prompt creation, workflow documentation.
- Familiarity with legal-tech platforms (Westlaw, LexisNexis, iManage) is a plus.
- Strong written/verbal communication; responsible handling of sensitive data.

### Core technical skills

Generative AI tools, prompt engineering, workflow automation; legal research/data platforms (Westlaw, LexisNexis, iManage); low-code/no-code development; document management / content curation; Microsoft Office and project-management software.
