## AI Software Engineer

### About the role

We are building agentic AI products for enterprise customers. You will ship LLM powered workflows to production with strong evaluation, safety, and observability practices.

### Required qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in CS, ML, or related field
- Production experience with **Python** backend services
- Hands on work with **LLMs** and **ML** systems in production
- **LangChain**, **LangGraph**, or similar **agentic** orchestration frameworks
- **RAG** pipelines: embeddings, vector search, retrieval evaluation
- **Cloud** deployment (Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, or GCP Vertex)
- **MLOps** basics: logging, tracing, model versioning, CI for ML services
- Ability to **explain technical concepts to non technical stakeholders** including product and compliance partners

### Preferred qualifications

- Fine tuning or evaluation of open source models (BERT, Llama, etc.)
- Experience with policy, safety, or human in the loop review workflows
- **SQL** for analytics on model outputs

### Responsibilities

- Design and implement multi step LLM workflows with reliable failure handling
- Build retrieval systems over document corpora; measure retrieval quality
- Partner with compliance and product to define acceptable model behavior
- Document architecture decisions and present tradeoffs to leadership
