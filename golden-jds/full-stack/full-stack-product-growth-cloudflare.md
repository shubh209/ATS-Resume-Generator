## Full Stack Engineer — Emerging Technologies & Incubation, Product Growth

### About Cloudflare

Cloudflare is building a better Internet through a global network that protects and accelerates
millions of websites and Internet properties, ranging from individual publishers and small
businesses to Fortune 500 companies. Its network improves performance and reduces spam and attacks
without requiring customers to install hardware or software or change application code.

Cloudflare values builders who identify normalized problems, use AI-native tools to create
solutions, iterate quickly, and share improvements with the wider team. The culture emphasizes
curiosity, practical shipping, and using AI as a partner in solving difficult Internet-scale
problems.

### About the role

The role sits within the Emerging Technologies & Incubation department and Product Growth team. It
bridges product engineering and growth, translating product vision into polished, high-performance
customer experiences that influence acquisition, engagement, retention, and other business
metrics across Cloudflare's self-service properties.

### Responsibilities

- Deliver customer-facing UI experiences and A/B experiments based on data-informed hypotheses to
  improve user acquisition, engagement, and retention.
- Architect scalable front-end solutions and build features throughout the product lifecycle,
  including front-end, back-end, infrastructure as code, system design, debugging, and testing.
- Partner with product, design, and data/analytics teams to define outcome measurements, establish
  experimentation frameworks, track behavioral attribution, and shape product roadmaps.
- Improve application performance, code quality, security, and developer productivity across core
  properties.
- Work with cross-functional teams to ensure product experiences comply with trust and safety
  principles.

### Desirable skills, knowledge, and experience

- Proficiency with TypeScript and JavaScript for reusable SDKs and UI components, plus strong HTML,
  CSS, and modern JavaScript ecosystem knowledge.
- Experience building high-scale serverless systems such as Cloudflare Workers, AWS Lambda, or
  Azure Functions.
- Experience designing interfaces around business KPIs such as onboarding and adoption, running
  A/B experiments, using feature flags or gating, and applying telemetry.
- Strong system-design fundamentals that balance engineering excellence with visual quality, plus
  experience using or designing RESTful APIs.
- Experience building tools or frameworks that improve developer productivity.
- Experience with modern AI/LLM-augmented development workflows and comfort in a fast-paced
  environment.

### Bonus points

- Familiarity with Go, Rust, or Python.
- Prior Cloudflare use or hands-on experience with Cloudflare Workers, Access, Zaraz, or cache
  configuration.
- Experience with commercial analytics platforms such as Google Analytics or Adobe Analytics.
