## Software Engineer — Intuit

### Company overview

Intuit is a global financial-technology platform serving tens of millions of customers through
TurboTax, Credit Karma, QuickBooks, and Mailchimp. Its products help consumers and small businesses
manage their finances.

### Job overview

This general posting is intended for candidates near the beginning of their professional careers,
with fewer than two years of industry experience. Intuit aligns successful candidates with teams
based on their skills, interests, and career goals.

Engineers work across cloud, platform, mobile, and SaaS products that serve consumers and small
businesses. The culture emphasizes innovation, experimentation, rapid prototyping, customer
collaboration, cross-functional teamwork, learning, and immediate product impact.

### Frontend engineering

- Develop scalable, responsive, and dynamic web applications.
- Work with React, AngularJS, Vue.js, or similar modern frontend frameworks.
- Apply HTML, CSS, and JavaScript standards.
- Collaborate with designers, UX specialists, and backend engineers.

### Backend engineering

- Develop web applications using Java, Python, Node.js, or another server-side language.
- Work with MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, or similar database technologies.
- Apply RESTful API design principles and integrate APIs.
- Use server frameworks such as Express, Spring MVC, or Django.

### Full-stack engineering

- Work across frontend and backend development.
- Discuss application and API architecture with developers and designers.
- Apply Agile methods and work with Scrum teams.
- Use development tools such as Git, Jenkins, or Travis.

### Mobile engineering

- Develop Android, iOS, or cross-platform mobile applications.
- Work with React Native, Flutter, or similar mobile frameworks.
- Understand tools such as Xcode and Android Studio and languages such as Objective-C, Swift, or
  Java.
- Integrate mobile applications with cloud-based APIs in collaboration with designers and backend
  engineers.

### Responsibilities across all stacks

- Contribute to core products, services, and systems that power critical engineering operations.
- Launch work that solves meaningful customer problems.
- Learn and apply proven practices for designing, building, and supporting software.
- Receive guidance, detailed feedback, and mentorship from experienced team members.
- Collaborate with peers across disciplines and build supportive working relationships.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor's or master's degree in Computer Science, a related technical discipline, or equivalent
  practical experience.
- Foundational understanding of Agile development, object-oriented design, and programming
  principles.
- Coursework, internships, personal projects, or academic work using languages such as Java,
  Python, JavaScript, or C++.
- Basic knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; familiarity with frontend frameworks is a plus.
- Strong written, verbal, teamwork, and collaboration skills.
- Awareness of AI concepts and basic understanding of capabilities such as generative AI.
- Willingness to learn and adapt to changing priorities, tools, and technologies.
