# YouTube Behavioral Interview Guidance

Purpose: Store external video transcripts and practical guidance used while refining the Amazon behavioral story bank.

## How to use this reference

- Treat creator claims about question frequency as opinion, not verified Amazon policy.
- Use the transcript to identify useful question types and answer techniques.
- Keep the job description and Amazon Leadership Principles files as the primary references.
- Do not copy sample answers. Apply only the guidance that fits the user's real experience.

## Guidance we are adopting

- Keep the situation to one or two simple sentences.
- State the goal clearly in the task section.
- In the action, explain what you did, why you did it, and how you handled it.
- Spend more time on decisions, people, tradeoffs, and judgment than technical implementation.
- Give a concrete result and explain what changed in your later behavior.
- Prepare for ambiguity, deadlines, calculated risk, root-cause analysis, work outside your responsibility, feedback, customer needs, and larger opportunities.

### Useful guidance from this transcript

- Prepare the introduction instead of improvising it.
- Use STAR so the interviewer can follow the answer and take clear notes.
- State why the situation mattered, but keep the context short.
- Clearly state the task or goal.
- In the action section, explain how you investigated, consulted people, made decisions, and presented the work.
- Include a specific lesson and describe how it changed later behavior.
- Ask thoughtful closing questions that create a real conversation.
- Prefer recent examples when they are strong and relevant.

### Guidance we reject or modify

- Do not exaggerate the size, risk, or importance of a situation. Use only facts that can survive follow-up questions.
- Do not spend two or three minutes explaining the situation and then summarize it. Give the simple summary first and keep the situation to one or two sentences.
- Do not add technical detail merely to prove that you know the implementation. Explain technical details only when they show judgment or answer a follow-up.
- Do not turn the interviewer's closing questions into another forced sales pitch. Ask a real question, listen, and connect your experience only when it fits naturally.
