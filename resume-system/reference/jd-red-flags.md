# JD Red Flags — Screening Before You Spend an Application

> Read-only reference. Check a posting against this list before tailoring a resume for it, not after.
> Purpose: don't spend an application slot, and don't spend tailoring effort, on a role likely to
> waste both.

---

## Workload red flags

| Phrase | What it usually signals |
|---|---|
| "Wear many hats" | Unclear scope, likely to absorb work outside the stated title |
| "Fast-paced environment" | Often fine, but paired with other flags below signals understaffing |
| "Hit the ground running" | Little to no onboarding support expected |
| "Self-starter in ambiguous situations" | Minimal direction; can be a genuine culture fit signal or a lack of management structure — check other flags before assuming either |

## Culture red flags

| Phrase | What it usually signals |
|---|---|
| "Rockstar" / "Ninja" / "Guru" | Vague expectations dressed as excitement; often correlates with immature hiring process |
| "We work hard, play hard" | Frequently paired with long hours normalized as culture |
| "Unlimited vacation" | Can be genuine, but sometimes correlates with no one actually taking time off |
| "Like a family" | Can signal blurred boundaries around hours/expectations |

## Compensation red flags

| Phrase | What it usually signals |
|---|---|
| "Competitive salary" (no range given) | Employer is not willing to disclose a number, which is a real signal in a market with pay-transparency norms |
| "Equity-heavy" | Often a substitute for low cash compensation — check the actual split |
| "Commission-based" (no stated base) | No income floor |
| "DOE" (depends on experience) with no range | Same as "competitive salary" — a placeholder for "we'd rather negotiate down" |

## Application-cost red flags

| Signal | What it usually means |
|---|---|
| Vague JD with no real qualifications section | Often means the requirements weren't thought through — hard to write a strong tailored match, and the actual hiring bar is unclear |
| Reposted listing with no changes | Can mean the previous hire didn't work out, or a stalled search — not automatically a skip, but worth noting |
| Explicit application quota mentioned (e.g. limited applications per period) | Don't burn a quota slot on a marginal-fit role — check the live skill's evidence bands and gaps first |

## What to do when a flag appears

A red flag is a **signal to weigh, not an automatic skip.** Two or more together, especially workload + compensation together, are the strongest combined signal to reconsider before spending tailoring effort. One flag alone (especially "competitive salary" with no range, which is extremely common) is often just industry-standard vagueness.

This list is descriptive pattern-matching, not a scoring formula — use judgment, and don't let it replace reading the actual posting.
